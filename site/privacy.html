Privacy Policy

Calculator Pro: Scientific · Effective September 30, 2026

Summary

Calculator Pro: Scientific does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet to process your calculations.

Data stored on your device

To remember your settings, stored variables, memory and calculation history between sessions, the app saves them locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app. You can also clear it at any time from SETUP → Reset all.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no data is shared with third parties.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
